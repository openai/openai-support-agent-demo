## Total Isolation for Safety and Maintenance
Isolator/Switch Disconnector Panels are designed to safely disconnect electrical circuits, providing total isolation for maintenance, repairs, or emergencies. ESL’s Isolator/Switch Disconnector Panels are engineered to provide total isolation, allowing for maintenance, repairs, or emergency shutdowns without risk of accidental re-energization. Equipped with Lock Out Tag Out (LOTO) mechanisms and robust overcurrent protection, these panels ensure that work can be done safely, even in the most demanding industrial environments. Designed for easy installation in both wall-mounted and floor-standing configurations, our panels are perfect for industries where safety and operational reliability are critical.
**Features:**
  * **Complete Circuit Isolation:** Ensures safe maintenance by disconnecting power.
  * **LOTO Capability:** Prevents accidental circuit reactivation during maintenance.
  * **Advanced Protection Features:** Safeguards against overcurrent and short circuits.
  * **Flexible Installation:** Available in both wall-mount and floor-standing configurations.


**Applications:** Ideal for industrial plants, commercial buildings, and facilities requiring regular maintenance and high safety standards.
**Quality Assurance:** Built according to IEC 61439 standards and ISO 9001 certified, ensuring maximum safety and reliability.
**Technical Data:**
  * Forms of Segregation: Available in Forms 1, 2, 3, and 4
  * Enclosure Type: Mild steel, stainless steel, polycarbonate
  * IP Rating: IP44 to IP66
  * Mounting Mode: Wall mount & floor standing
