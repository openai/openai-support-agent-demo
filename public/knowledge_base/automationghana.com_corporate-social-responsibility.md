## We are passionate about people, not just our people, but everyone who interacts with us: our community and beyond.
Driven by our core values of “PEOPLE DEVELOPMENT” and integrity, TAGG commits to meaningful social impact programs aimed at improving lives and transforming our communities. Our CSR programs span education, healthcare, environmental protection, sustainability, and social change.
## Our Interventions
![The Automation Ghana Group - photo 2023 12 08 10 40 03](https://automationghana.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/photo_2023-12-08_10-40-03.jpg)
Future Leaders' Educational Fund 
In 2015, PPA launched the Future Leaders' Educational Fund with the objective of keeping children from underprivileged homes in school, offering them the opportunity of a brighter future 
[ Learn more ](https://automationghana.com/future-leaders-educational-fund/)
![The Automation Ghana Group - photo 2025 06 24 16 31 59](https://automationghana.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/photo_2025-06-24_16-31-59.jpg)
STEM Club 
As part of our unwavering commitment to sustainability and social impact, we believe that true progress is measured not just by economic growth, but by the lives we uplift along the way. 
[ Learn more ](https://automationghana.com/stem-club/)
![The Automation Ghana Group - overspeeding Kills](https://automationghana.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/overspeeding-Kills.jpg)
Social Impact 
Road Safety Campaign and Sanitation Campaign 
[ Learn more ](https://automationghana.com/social-impact/)
![The Automation Ghana Group - photo 2025 06 24 16 51 29](https://automationghana.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/photo_2025-06-24_16-51-29.jpg)
Breast Cancer Support 
TAGG supports breast cancer screening, testing, and sensitization through an NGO. Through donations, screening equipment is purchased, and outreach programs are organized to bring screening, testing, and awareness close to those who cannot afford it. 
[ Learn more ](https://automationghana.com/breast-cancer-support/)
![The Automation Ghana Group - IMG 5571 scaled](https://automationghana.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/IMG_5571-scaled.jpg)
Graduate Trainee Program 
Since 2016, a significant number of fresh graduates have been enrolled, with an over 95% success rate in securing jobs after the program. TAGG has absorbed about 60% of the graduate trainees since its inception in 2016. 
[ Learn more ](https://automationghana.com/graduate-trainee-program/)
