## Reliable Distribution Boards for Safe and Efficient Power Management
**Distribution Boards** are the heart of every electrical system, responsible for safely dividing and distributing power across circuits. A well-designed distribution board ensures reliable power supply, protects equipment, and enhances safety for both industrial and commercial facilities.
At Automation Ghana, we provide **custom-built, high-quality distribution boards** engineered to meet your power requirements and safety standards.
###  Our Distribution Board Solutions
We specialize in a wide range of **power distribution boards** , including:
  * **Main Distribution Boards (MDBs)** – Centralized power control for large facilities.
  * **Sub-Distribution Boards (SDBs)** – Efficient secondary power distribution for specific sections or departments.
  * **Low Voltage Distribution Boards (LVDBs)** – Ideal for safe, controlled distribution in industrial and commercial setups.
  * **Custom-Built Distribution Boards** – Tailored solutions to match unique load and safety requirements.


### Key Features Of Our Distribution Boards 
  * Robust design for industrial and commercial applications
  * High-quality circuit breakers and protective devices
  * Flexible configurations (wall-mounted, floor-standing)
  * Compliance with international electrical standards
  * Easy accessibility for operation and maintenance
